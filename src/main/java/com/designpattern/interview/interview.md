## Most important interview pattern

For your experience level, don't prepare only:

> **“What is Kafka?”**

Prepare the complete chain:

> **What is Kafka? → Why did you use Kafka? → Where did you use it? → How does it work? → What happens if Kafka goes down? → What happens if a message is duplicated? → How do you retry? → How do you handle failed messages? → How do you maintain ordering? → How do you monitor consumer lag?**

The same pattern applies to **Redis, Microservices, Circuit Breaker, JWT, JPA, MySQL, MongoDB, AWS and Docker**.

This is the level of preparation I would use for a **3–5 year Java/Spring Boot Microservices L1 interview**.
